Hyperscaler

💡 A hyperscaler is a cloud and internet infrastructure provider that can rapidly scale compute, storage, and networking to massive, global demand. Key characteristics: huge data-center footprint, high automation and standardization, elastic on-demand scaling, multi-region global networks, and continuous cost and reliability optimization. The top 5 globally are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, and Tencent Cloud.
