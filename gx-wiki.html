The grammar

Every G3x service-module PN follows one pattern. Learn the shape once and most part numbers read themselves.

prefix
What kind of item this is — a service module, a chassis, a pluggable, a cable.
fn
Form factor: slot width and which chassis family it seats in.
ZZ
Functionality — ROADM, Raman pump, add/drop, amplifier, transponder.

Port count, ROADM degrees, channel count or gain range.

Performance and variant letters — WSS type, gain class, amplified, expandable.

Transmission band, and whether it can be upgraded later.
v
Version digit — hardware revision. 0 is the first release.
The middle block is always 8 characters, padded with Z. G1RPBM + ZZ, OMD32E + ZZ, G31CHAS + Z, C2OCC2E + Z. The trailing Zs carry no meaning — they pad the field to a fixed width. This is what lets the decoder above find the boundary between the port code and the variant letter, and it is why so many PNs appear to have a stray “ZZ” in the middle. (Derived from the part-number lists, not stated in the deck — one PN, G3S-G2RPB4M-Y2, breaks it at 7.)
Cables use a different tail — and the sources disagree on it. On a GVS- cable the final digits are length, not band and version. The naming deck says length in cm/10, which makes -03 = 30 cm. But the Aug-2026 part list describes GVS-PCC15AUZ-03 as a 3 m cable. Read the description rather than the code until PLM confirms which rule is live.

Chassis

Two series. The letters in a chassis name are not decoration: they tell you depth, backplane and slot height.

Reading a chassis name Lowercase c = compact, 300 mm depth (G34c, G32c). X = backplane “Xross” pairing (G34Xc). E = enables double-height slots (G32E) — which is why RD66 only goes there. The second-digit rule does not apply to the G20 series.

Rows × slots and RU come from the V3.0 AUX sheet where the tool models the chassis, and from the Aug-2026 roadmap deck for G32c, G34L and G38c, which post-date an R9.1 tool. Depth per the naming deck: the full family is 600 mm, the compact family 300 mm.

Sled families

A sled name is a category prefix, a width or generation digit, then letters that qualify it.

PrefixFull nameRole
The digit after the category prefix means two different things On the G30 series it is slot width in quarter-sleds — CHM1 is one, CHM2 is two. On the G40 series it is the coherent generation — CHM6 is ICE-6, CHM7 is ICE-7, CHM8 is ICE-8 — or capacity, as in UCM4 = 400G. Same position, different meaning, depending on the shelf.

Configuration rules

What the V3.0 configurator enforces when it builds a shelf — the part a naming convention cannot tell you.

Slot cost per card

PNSled nameSlotsClass
Straight from the V3.0 AUX sled table. Chassis quantity derives from these, so they are the numbers that decide how many shelves a design needs.

OMDs are shelves, not cards

V3.0 gives every OMD its own chassis type with its own rack units and no slot cost. A mux-terminated site therefore does not spend a termination slot on the OMD — it adds a separate passive shelf to the rack.

PEM and breaker sizing

Both Nokia tools agree on 90 % V3.0's own release notes state "PEMs input power assumes 90 % efficiency, AC input is based on 220 V AC". The PowerDraw breaker sheet uses the same 0.90, a minimum voltage of 40.5 V and a 0.85 breaker derate. PEM count is ⌈input ÷ 1300 W⌉ for the G3x shelves and ⌈input ÷ 2200 W⌉ for the G42, doubled for 1+1 or plus one for 1:N.
